# B2 Motorsports driver bio questionnaire

Used for the "Our Drivers" page (`/about-us/drivers/`). Every driver gets the same questions so
the cards line up. The six short answers fill the card's fields; the rest feed the bio line and
future blog posts. Copy everything below the line into an email.

---

**Subject:** Your bio for the B2 Motorsports website

Hi [first name],

We are building an "Our Drivers" page on the B2 Motorsports website and we would like you on it.
The point of the page is to show that racing is not only about winning: everyone has their own
goals and gets something different out of it. So we are more interested in why you race than in
your results. Short answers are fine; a sentence or two each is plenty. Skip anything you would
rather not answer. We will edit lightly for length and send it back to you before anything goes up.

1. **Your name as you want it shown.** First and last, or first name only.
2. **How did you get started in racing?** Karts, sim, a track day, a friend, a parent?
3. **How many years have you been racing?** And the year of your first race, if you know it.
4. **Why do you race?** What do you get out of it that you don't get anywhere else?
5. **What does a good weekend look like for you?** It might be a lap time, a clean race, a battle you won, or a day with your family in the paddock.
6. **What is your goal right now?** A skill, a lap time, a license, a season goal, or just more seat time.
7. **What moment are you proudest of?** It does not have to be a win. A first finish, a clean race or a pass you worked for all weekend all count.
8. **Where is your home track, or your favorite?** And why, in a sentence.
9. **Where are you from, and what do you do away from the track?** City and state, and your day job or anything else you want to share.
10. **One line about racing with B2.** What it is like, or why you picked us.
11. **Advice for someone thinking about their first race?** One or two sentences.

**Photo.** Please attach one or two photos you like, in or next to the car. A sharp phone photo
is fine. Tell us who took each one so we can credit them, and confirm you have the right to share
it.

**Permission.** Please reply with this line, filled in:

> I agree that B2 Motorsports may publish my name, photo and the answers above on
> b2motorsports.com and B2 Motorsports social media. I can ask for changes or removal at any time
> by emailing info@b2motorsports.com.
>

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian needs to send the permission line.

## Notes for B2 (not for the email)

- Card fields on the page: How I started (Q2), Years racing (Q3), Home track (Q8), Why I race (Q4),
  Proudest moment (Q7), My goal right now (Q6). Question 10 is the italic line under the card.
- Question 5 (a good weekend) often makes the best pull quote; use it in the card line or on social.
- Questions 9 and 11 are optional and are not shown on the card yet; keep them for blog posts and
  social.
- Keep the emphasis on goals and satisfaction, not results. Results are fine in the "proudest
  moment" answer if that is what the driver chose.
- Do not publish a driver until the signed permission line is back. File it with the photo in
  SharePoint (Marketing Materials › Photos & Video) under the driver's name.
- Do not show a minor's last name without the parent's say-so.
