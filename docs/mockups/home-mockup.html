What a weekend costs

~$4,500
typical two-day track weekend at Barbernot on site yet

A typical two-day track weekend runs around $4,500. Race weekends are priced line by line.

Six lines, nothing hidden. Transport and prep are charged once per event, so the second day costs less than the first. Tires and fuel are estimated up front and settled on what you actually use. The crash-damage deposit is $6,000, returned in full if the car comes back undamaged.
