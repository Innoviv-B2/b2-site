Using the equipment

Three rules that keep it working.

  • ✓Reservations win

    Lifts, the alignment rack and the dyno are by reservation. Unreserved time is first come, but a reservation wins.

  • ✓Barrier for shared rows

    No work on a car in a shared row without a barrier; welding and grinding by arrangement.

  • ✓Ask when you reach your limit

    The crew is on the other side of the same wall. Non-member lift time is $25 an hour by reservation, and owner services take over where you want them to.
