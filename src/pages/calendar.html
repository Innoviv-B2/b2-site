How it works

Three steps get you on the trailer.

The same process for every event on the table below, whether it is a test day or a national race.

Step 1

Register with the organizer

Use the Register link on the row for your event. Test days and open practice need no competition license.

Step 2

Text us your dates

We confirm your trailer spot and where your car sits in the schedule that weekend.

Step 3

We send the itemized quote

Transport, tires, fuel, car rental, and trackside support, line by line.

  • License column: None = anyone with track experience · Comp = SCCA competition license or novice permit · SVRA or Comp = SVRA vintage license or current comp license + medical.
  • Time Trials entry needs a Time Trials license only, a quick online application with no racing school required.
  • Test days and open practice are open without a competition license.
  • SIC races also require two SARRC weekends entered in the same season, no exceptions. Reply and we'll check your season.
