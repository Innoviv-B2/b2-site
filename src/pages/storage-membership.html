Good to know

How it works day to day.

  • ✓24/7 access

    Two-factor entry on the door, cameras inside and out. Members come and go on their own schedule.

  • ✓Family included

    The family's cars can live here on one membership, and your family comes with you: your spouse or partner and your kids are covered.

  • ✓Guests

    Bring a guest vehicle up to four times a year, $25 per visit. People you bring are welcome whenever you are in the building with them.

  • ✓Book your time

    Lifts and the alignment rack can be booked ahead, so the time is yours when you arrive. Anything not booked is open to whoever is in the building. Dyno time is scheduled ahead.

  • ✓Insurance is yours

    You insure the car; we recommend a cover, a drip pan and a fire extinguisher within one space of it.

  • ✓Barrier for shared rows

    No work on a car in a shared row without a barrier; welding and grinding by arrangement.

  • ✓Twelve months, then month to month

    The first term is twelve months; after that it runs month to month, and you give us 30 days' notice when you want to leave. Payment by ACH, agreement by DocuSign.

  • ✓Members who stay

    The twelve-month first term keeps turnover low. The people in the spaces next to yours are the same people next year, the garage is looked after by people who plan to be here, and you know who you are sharing a lift with.
