Good to know

How it works day to day.

  • ✓24/7 access

    Two-factor entry on the door, cameras inside and out. Members come and go on their own schedule.

  • ✓Family included

    Your membership covers your spouse or partner and your kids. Bring a guest up to twelve times a year.

  • ✓Reservations win

    Lifts and the alignment rack are by reservation. Unreserved time is first come, but a reservation wins.

  • ✓Insurance is yours

    You insure the car; we recommend a cover, a drip pan and a fire extinguisher within one space of it.

  • ✓Barrier for shared rows

    No work on a car in a shared row without a barrier; welding and grinding by arrangement.

  • ✓Month to month

    Month to month after the first term. Payment by ACH, agreement by DocuSign. needs Cliff: length of the first term, and notice to leave
