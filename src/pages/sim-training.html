How it works

We connect you with the coaches and the rig, not the other way around

B2 built and ran its own racing simulator for years, and simulator hardware development is part of our history: pedal assemblies and setups tuned for the transition from an SRF3 to a TA2 car, tested by our own drivers. That in-house rig is out of commission today.

What has not changed is the goal: a driver who shows up at the track already knowing the corners, the braking points and the reference points, instead of spending the first session of a track day just learning the layout. We now get you there a different way. We connect you with the sim coaches we trust for structured, purposeful sessions, and we help you choose and set up the right simulator rig at home, sized to what you actually need, not the most expensive box on the shelf.

What it is for

Learn the track before the trailer ever leaves the shop

Sim time is cheap compared to track time, and it is where a lot of the real learning happens before you ever turn a wheel in the car.

  • ✓New tracks

    Walk the corners, the braking points and the elevation changes before your first laps on a track you have never seen.

  • ✓Race craft

    Starts, traffic, and decision-making under pressure, repeatable in a way a real track weekend never is.

  • ✓Off-season sharpness

    Keep the reflexes and the reference points fresh between events, without the cost of a track day.
