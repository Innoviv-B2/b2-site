Section 1 · The network

Sim and real-world coaching, through our network.

Racing simulator training only works when the coach has done it for real. The coaches in the B2 network have, and each can work in the sim and at the track.

Coaching runs in the sim and at the track, from a strengths-and-weaknesses conversation and a track walk to a data lap and session-by-session video and data review. Tell us your goal and we will make the introduction.

What it is for

Learn the track before the trailer ever leaves the shop.

A coached hour in the rig removes the first-session-of-the-weekend problem: you arrive knowing the corners, the braking points and the references.

  • ✓New tracks

    Barber, Road Atlanta, Sebring and the rest of the calendar, learned before your first real lap.

  • ✓Race craft

    Starts, traffic and decisions under pressure, repeated in a way a real weekend never allows.

  • ✓Off-season

    Reflexes and reference points kept sharp between events without the cost of a track day.

  • ✓Your own rig

    We help you choose and set up a home simulator sized to what you need, not the most expensive box on the shelf.

Section 2 · Sim time

Sim time at The Golf Lab’s Race Lounge.

B2’s own simulator is not in service. For sim racing we partner with The Golf Lab’s Race Lounge: we refer sim customers to them, and we coach in their facility. Their Race Lounge page lists the cars and tracks, racing leagues, and a Book Race Time button.
