What a Hoosier Super Tour season costs in our SRF3

The Hoosier Super Tour is SCCA's national series: nine weekends in 2026, two races each, and a Full Competition License to enter. The five eastern rounds in our Spec Racer Ford Gen3 come to about $35,800 with the entries, for 21 hours on track.

Every weekend is priced line by line below, with the published entry fee beside it.

How we worked out these numbers

Calculator inputs: two cars in the trailer; two-day weekends 3 h over 6 sessions, three-day weekends 5 h over 8 sessions; scuffed tires; transport, prep, trackside crew per day, tires, fuel and the car by the track hour, with crew hotel nights included where the tow runs over eight hours each way. VIR, Mid-Ohio and Watkins Glen distances are estimates (±15%) until we quote. Entries are the published 2026 fees plus the $30 SRF3 compliance fee: Road Atlanta $795, VIR $795 (Atlanta Region and NCR supplementary regulations), Mid-Ohio $750 (OVR supplementary regulations), Watkins Glen $950 (Finger Lakes Region registration), Barber $795 (Tennessee Valley Region registration). Before you enter

What the Super Tour asks of you

You need a Full Competition License to enter, and not every round has a test day. Barber's has none, which is why the seat time before your first round matters more than the license. In 2026 our driver Ryan Starck ran the tour in fields of 38 to 47 cars and finished between 14th and 39th, with his best, 14th of 40, at Barber in August. Paths Into Racing shows the seat time that comes before a weekend like that and what it costs, and the license page covers the credential.

What comes with the car at every round

We transport, prep, fuel and run the car, and the same crew sets it up from Road Atlanta in March to Barber in August, so they remember what you asked for last time. Coaching is included at race weekends: data, video and a plan for the next session, between every session. You drive to the track with your gear.

Our drivers have run the tour in our cars. Two weekends, written up: Mid-Ohio, a step forward for Jackson Diehl, and the June Sprints at Road America.

Thinking about a season? Ask before you price it.

Ask about multi-race discounts when you plan a season; they depend on the size of the commitment and the terms. Tell us which rounds you want and we'll come back with the season costed, and any discount that applies.
