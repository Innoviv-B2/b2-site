What a weekend costs

~$4,500

Two-day track weekend · Barber · 8 sessions · 4 h on track

A typical two-day track weekend runs around $4,500. Race weekends are priced line by line.

Six lines, nothing hidden: transport, prep, trackside support, tires, fuel and car rental. Transport and prep are charged once per event, so the second day costs less than the first. Tires and fuel are estimated up front and settled on what you actually use. The crash-damage deposit is $6,000, returned in full if the car comes back undamaged.
