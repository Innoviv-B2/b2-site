Why race activation

Why race activation works.

Road racing gives a brand or a host something a conference room cannot.

  • ✓Rigor

    The effort the team, drivers and partners put into the result is visible all weekend. Race weekends run 13-plus hour days in the heat and the sun, chasing a result that is never guaranteed. Guests feel that energy up close.

  • ✓Adrenaline

    The car crossing the finish line, the engine starting under the tent, and for guests who want it, the thrill of driving a car themselves.

  • ✓Community

    Family, friends and neighbors working toward one goal. It is the cookout after the long day and the camaraderie that builds lasting relationships.

  • ✓Engagement

    A unique way to spend real time with your customers, away from a desk or a screen.

What to expect

What can you expect at a B2 Motorsports experience?

The B2 team works with you to build a day that fits your business, your prospects, your clients or your employees. The experience starts before you reach the track: every guest gets an itinerary for the weekend, a map of points of interest, and the contact information for their hosts. When you arrive, your host meets you at the gate and walks you to home base. Through the weekend, your host answers questions about the cars, the track and racing, and shares the passion for it with you and your guests.
