Getting started

How to get a racing license: the SCCA route, step by step

There are two routes to an SCCA license, they cost very different amounts, and four of the rules changed recently enough that much of the advice online is out of date. Here is the current path, what each step costs, and the lowest-cost routes to a license, including the ones that are not us. One thing first: a license and being ready to race are two different things. This page is about the license; Paths Into Racing is about the rest.

What you can do today, without one

The license only gates wheel-to-wheel racing. Plenty of real track time needs nothing more than a driver's license, and all of it counts toward getting ready.

No membership

Track days and open lapping

Track Night in America runs about $160, and organizers like Chin Track Days and the Talladega GP track events take you with nothing but a driver's license. No clock, no passing pressure: laps.

SCCA membership

Time Trials

Membership and a driver's license get you a Time Trials Novice license and a real clock, still with no wheel-to-wheel. In a purpose-built race car like the SRF3 you wear the full kit; the gear post has the details.

What changed

Four things most advice online still gets wrong

01

You no longer need a Novice Permit to attend a drivers' school.

Changed September 2025. Membership and a state driver's license are enough. SCCA calls it “try before you buy”: you can find out whether you like this before you buy the credential.

02

Under 60? You no longer need a doctor's visit.

From 1 July 2026 it is an online Declaration of Medical Fitness, annual, $0. The February 2026 GCR text still describes the old physician requirement and has not caught up; the June 2026 Medical Examination Packet is the current word.

03

Time Trials do not earn you a Novice Permit.

This is the one that costs people a season. Time Trials cannot substitute for the drivers' school requirement at all. Under 2026 GCR AppC.2.7.F, a Time Trial replaces exactly one of the three regional race weekends, and only after you already hold the permit. One. Not three.

04

The club lane is three weekends now, not two.

Recently raised. If someone told you “school plus two races,” they learned it under the old rule.

Sources: SCCA drivers' school page and accreditation table; 2026 GCR AppC.2.7.F; June 2026 Medical Examination Packet. All read 15 Sep 2026.

The part after the paperwork

Getting good is the hard part.

Look at what you are holding when you walk out of any school: a school certificate for a license, and somewhere between three and ten hours of track time. Getting a license is the easy part. Getting good is the hard part.

Nobody is ready for a Hoosier Super Tour the Monday after a license school. A Novice Permit already lets you race regionals, and a Full Competition License lets you enter a Major or a Super Tour, but neither one says whether you will be quick, whether you can hold a line with a car alongside, or whether you know what the car is telling you in the third session of a hot Saturday. Those come from hours.

The Barber Hoosier Super Tour costs $795 to enter, requires a Full Competition License, and has no test day. Turning up there with eight hours of experience is not a plan.

The better question is how you become a racer. Everyone's path there is different, and progression is not linear. That is what we are for: a fun, family-friendly paddock where you learn at your own pace, with a crew and coaches who have done this a long time. The license is one step along the way.

The Club Racing Experience needs no license to enter, and it earns drivers' school credit through the Alternative Drivers' School process once you reach three hours of track time and an instructor signs you off (GCR AppC.2.7.E.3). SCCA's own guidance says you may need two or three CRE weekends to get there. The catch is geographic: the only region running them regularly is New England, and the single national Race Experience event scheduled for 2026, in Arizona, was cancelled. If you are in the Southeast, this route does not exist this year. If you find a SEDIV CRE on a 2026 calendar, tell us and we will update this page.
