Club racing, on camera. Amateur Racer on YouTube.

B2 Motorsports partners with the production company Ready To Air to make Amateur Racer, a documentary series about the people who race SCCA club events on their own time and their own dime. If you race with us, you may end up in it.

The series

Made with Ready To Air.

Ready To Air is a television production company led by executive producer and director Gianclaudio "GC" Angelini. Together with B2 we film the paddock the way it actually is: the drivers, the crews, the long tow, the eight-minute qualifying session that decides a weekend. The Amateur Racer YouTube channel is where the episodes land.

If you race with B2

Your weekend, told properly.

Drivers who rent with us or run with the team at a filmed event can be part of an episode. It is a documentary, not an advertisement, so the camera follows the weekend as it happens.

  • ✓On camera

    Interviews, in-car and trackside footage from the weekends Ready To Air covers. An appearance release is signed beforehand.

  • ✓Shareable

    A published episode is something to send to a sponsor, a family, or an employer who wonders what you do on weekends.

  • ✓Sponsor exposure

    A partner's name on a B2 car appears in the episodes the car is in. See business & hospitality.

  • ·Not every weekend

    Filming follows the production schedule, not the calendar. Ask which events are being covered when you book.

Race weekend photos

For still photography we use Appleton Automotive Images, a Huntsville-based motorsports photographer whose work covers Road Atlanta, Sebring, Barber and the short tracks around North Alabama.
