B2 Motorsports partners with the production company Ready To Air to make Amateur Racer, a documentary series about the people who race SCCA club events on their own time and their own dime. Racing with us, you can add a film of your own weekend.

The series

Made with Ready To Air.

Ready To Air is a television production company led by executive producer and director Gianclaudio "GC" Angelini. Together with B2 we film the paddock the way it actually is: the drivers, the crews, the long tow, the eight-minute qualifying session that decides a weekend. The Amateur Racer YouTube channel is where the episodes land.

Add-on · your weekend on film

Your weekend, told properly.

Add driver-specific content to any weekend with us. We have partnered with Ready To Air to produce it: we bring the cameras and capture your weekend, and Ready To Air edits the raw footage into your story. needs Cliff: price, or "quoted per weekend"

  • ✓The kit

    A DJI Osmo Pocket 3, Meta smart glasses, Insta360 GO 3S cameras and the AiM SmartyCams in the cars. In-car, on the driver, in the paddock and on the grid.

  • ✓We capture it

    The crew runs the cameras through the weekend alongside the normal job, so you drive and we film.

  • ✓Ready To Air edits it

    The raw footage goes to Ready To Air, who cut it to match your story: a first race, a license weekend, a season in the car.

  • ✓Yours to share

    Something to send to a sponsor, a family, or an employer who wonders what you do on weekends. A partner's name on your car is in every frame. See business & hospitality.
