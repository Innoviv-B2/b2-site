Our drivers · Huntsville, AL

Meet the B2 Motorsports drivers.

Racing is not only about winning. Every driver here is chasing something different, and gets something different out of it. Here is who races with us, and why, in their own words.

Only one driver wins each race. If that were the only reason to do this, almost nobody would. The B2 Motorsports drivers did not all start in the same place and they are not all chasing the same thing. One wants to take half a second out of a corner that has bothered them all year. One wants a clean race from green to checker, or the first finish in the top half of the field. One races because it is the one weekend a month the whole family spends together in the paddock. One is building toward a national championship. All of those count, and all of them are personal.

Every driver on this page answered the same short set of questions: how they started, why they race, what they are working toward right now, and the moment they are proudest of, which is often not a trophy. We will add more as bios come in. If you are thinking about joining them, start with how arrive-and-drive works and check whether you fit the car.

Your race, wherever you are on the grid

In a Spec Racer, there is always someone to race.

The cars are sealed and equal, and the fields are some of the biggest in club racing. That means there is a battle at every position, not just at the front. Wherever your pace is today, there is a driver right next to it, and that fight for twelfth is every bit as real as the fight for the win.

A better lap

The clock does not care where you finished. Beating your own best lap is a win you can repeat all season.

A clean race

Green to checker, no contact, every decision your own. Harder than it sounds and worth being proud of.

Your battle

The driver you have been chasing for three weekends. Getting past them, and holding them off, is the best part of a race.

The weekend itself

Friends and family under the awning, a paddock that looks after its own, and the drive home talking it over.

How the bios work

Same questions for every driver

Short answers are fine. We edit lightly for length and send it back to you before anything goes up.

Step 1

Answer eleven short questions

How you started, why you race, what a good weekend looks like for you, your goal right now, the moment you are proudest of, and a line about racing with B2.

Step 2

Send a photo

One you like, in or next to the car. A phone photo is fine if it is sharp. Tell us who took it so we can credit them.

Step 3

Say yes, in writing

Nothing about you goes on the site until you have read it and told us it can. You can ask us to change it or take it down at any time.
