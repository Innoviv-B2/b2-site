Our drivers · Huntsville, AL

Meet the B2 Motorsports drivers.

Sim racers, karters, first-timers, and a father who has been at it since 1976. Here is who races with us, in their own words.

The B2 Motorsports drivers did not all start in the same place. Some came up through karts. Some came from iRacing and wanted to find out whether the sim had taught them anything real. Some had never been on a track before their first test day in a Spec Racer Ford. What they share is a car that rewards learning, a crew that shows them the bill, and a paddock where the person next to you will lend you a torque wrench.

Every driver on this page answered the same short set of questions: how they started, how long they have been racing, what they drive, where they like to race, the result they are proudest of and what they are working on now. We will add more as bios come in. If you are thinking about joining them, start with how arrive-and-drive works and check whether you fit the car.

How the bios work

Same questions for every driver

Short answers are fine. We edit lightly for length and send it back to you before anything goes up.

Step 1

Answer ten questions

How you started, years racing, your car and class, home track, proudest result, what you are working on, and a line about racing with B2.

Step 2

Send a photo

One you like, in or next to the car. A phone photo is fine if it is sharp. Tell us who took it so we can credit them.

Step 3

Say yes, in writing

Nothing about you goes on the site until you have read it and told us it can. You can ask us to change it or take it down at any time.

Race with B2? Send us your bio.

Email us and we will send the questions, or just reply with your answers and a photo. Not racing yet? Read about the paths into racing our drivers took, or start a conversation.
