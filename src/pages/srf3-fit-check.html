Spec Racer Ford Gen3 · Fit check

Fit is about proportions, not size.

Whether you fit an SRF3 comes down to leg room, shoulder room and wheel clearance, and all three adjust. Height and weight on their own tell us very little. A real Spec Racer Ford fit check looks at proportions together, not a single number.

For example: we have fitted a driver at 6'7" and 275 lb. That is not a limit. Someone taller or heavier with different proportions can fit just as well; someone shorter can need more adjustment. We look at the numbers together and set the car up for you.

  • SeatStandard or custom insert, foam or bead.
  • PedalsRack slides, extended set available.
  • WheelColumn tilts and extends, smaller wheel available.
  • BeltsHarness and head-and-neck sized to you; arm restraints and HANS provided.

How we check

What happens after you send your numbers.

Step 1

You send five measurements.

Height, weight, seated height, shoulder width, helmet size. Two minutes, no account.

Step 2

A person reads them.

We compare your numbers to drivers we have actually fitted. If it is a clear yes, you hear that. If it is a maybe, you hear what we would change: insert, pedals, wheel.

Step 3

Sit in one if you want to be sure.
