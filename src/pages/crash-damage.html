Spec Racer Ford crash damage: what it actually costs.

Almost nobody in this business publishes damage terms. Most say the driver is "responsible for damages" and stop there. We would rather you know before you book: the deposit, how repairs are billed, and what the incidents we actually see tend to cost.

See common incidents Price a weekend

The deposit

$6,000, returned in full if the car comes back undamaged.

The crash-damage deposit is paid before the event with the rental. Most weekends it comes straight back. If something is damaged, we repair it or have it repaired, bill the actual cost including any freight, and apply the deposit to that bill: if the repair costs less you get the difference back, and if it costs more you owe the difference.

  • Deposit$6,000, paid before the event
  • Undamaged carReturned in full
  • DamageActual repair cost, parts and freight, billed against the deposit
  • Normal wearOurs, not yours
  • Your responsibilityContact, an off-track excursion, an over-rev, abusive shifting, or driving on past a warning light

How to keep it at zero

Most damage is avoidable, and the cheapest repair is the one that never happens. Read the Intro to the Spec Racer binder before your weekend: it covers start-up, the gearbox and over-rev, the mistakes that most often damage a car. Build up to the limit across a weekend rather than in the first session, and use the coaching between sessions.
