Spec Racer Ford crash damage: what it actually costs.

Almost nobody in this business publishes damage terms. Most say the driver is "responsible for damages" and stop there. We would rather you know before you book: who pays for what, the deposit, and how the repair of each incident we actually see is billed.

The deposit

$6,000, held while you race with us.

Damage you cause is yours to pay for. A mechanical failure that is not your fault is not, and the cases in between we work out with you, as a team, walking through the cost and the cause. The $6,000 crash deposit is paid before your first event and we hold it for as long as you race with us, so it does not move back and forth each weekend; it comes back when you are done. It is security against what you owe, not a limit on it: the most you can owe is the entire car. If something is damaged, we repair it or have it repaired and bill the actual cost, including any freight, against the deposit.

  • Deposit$6,000, paid before your first event
  • How long we hold itWhile you race with us; returned when you are done
  • YoursDamage you cause: contact, an off-track excursion, an over-rev, abusive shifting, or driving on past a warning light
  • Not yoursA mechanical failure that is not your fault, and normal wear
  • In betweenWorked out with you, as a team
  • BilledActual repair cost and any freight, against the deposit
  • Most you can oweThe entire car; the deposit is security, not a limit

How to keep it at zero

Most damage is avoidable, and the cheapest repair is the one that never happens. Read the Intro to the Spec Racer binder before your weekend: it covers start-up, the gearbox and over-rev, the mistakes that most often damage a car. Build up to the limit across a weekend rather than in the first session, and use the coaching between sessions.
