What it is

Not a class. A path.

Every driver who comes through B2 is somewhere different: never turned a wheel on track, a handful of track days in, licensed and looking to get faster, or already racing and chasing a national result. The driver development program is how we meet you where you are and move you forward one step at a time, instead of selling everyone the same package.

Step 1

First track days

Car control, lines, and building the habit of driving smoothly before driving fast. This is where most of the bad habits get fixed, or never form.

Step 2

Competition license

A structured route to a Full Competition License, and what to do with the time in between the classroom and the exam. See the license page for the routes we recommend.

Step 3

Race weekends

Race pace, starts, traffic and strategy, with Cliff at the track. See what it costs to get here.

Step 4

Competition & beyond

National-level events, a longer season, and the data habits that keep shaving tenths once the big gains are gone.

Coaching

Coaching, not a coaching tier

Coaching is not something you pay extra for once you are already committed. At race weekends, Cliff is at the track with you, walking the session plan, watching the sector times and talking through the radio when it matters. At track days and time trials, coaching is arranged in advance, so we know to have someone free to work with you before you arrive.

Either way, the goal is the same: fewer wasted sessions, and a clear answer to "what do I work on next."

Data & video review

Every session, reviewed in plain language

After every session, we pull the data and, where we have it, the video, and go through it with you in plain language: not a spreadsheet dump, a short conversation about the two or three things that will actually make the next session faster. Over a weekend those conversations compound. Over a season they turn into a driver who knows what to look for without being told.

  • ✓Sector and lap-time comparison

    Session to session, and against the car's own best laps.

  • ✓Braking points and traffic

    Where time is actually being lost, not where it feels like it is.

  • ✓One clear focus for next time

    Not a list of ten things. The one adjustment that matters most right now.

Where this leads

Renting, owning, or somewhere in between

Most drivers in the program are renting from us: an SRF3, prepped, transported and crewed, so the only thing you have to think about is driving. See how a weekend works on Race With Us. If you already own a Spec Racer, the same coaching and data review come with it, and a weekend in your own car is priced like a rental minus the car: the calculator has a switch for it. Either way, you keep the same coach and the same crew from one event to the next.
