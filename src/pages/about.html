About B2 Motorsports · Huntsville, AL

A family operation, thirty years in the paddock.

We put people in Spec Racer Fords and stand behind the wall with them. Same crew, same awning, every weekend. Here is who we are and how we got here.

The name

Why B2

Three reasons, and all of them are true. You can Be a driver Too: racing is not only for people on television. Business to Business: our customers help each other turn a passion into something that pays for itself, and we make the introductions. And Boom-Boom, the name Cliff earned with a crash at Road Atlanta in 2001, his rookie Spec Racer season. It was not the only crash that year, but it was the one that stuck, and Mark Eaton still brings it up.

Two generations, in one column

How we got here

  • 1976

    Cliff's father, Wade White, starts racing A and B Production Corvettes in the SCCA.

  • 1990

    Cliff starts racing quarter midgets, age five, with the Huntsville Quarter Midget Association.

  • Late 1990s

    Karts: WKA road racing and sprint racing, including the track that is now Music City Karting.

  • 2001

    First Spec Racer Ford season. SCCA Southeast Division Rookie of the Year. Also more than a few crashes, including the one at Road Atlanta that made him Boom-Boom.

  • 2002

    SCCA SRF Pro Series Rookie of the Year. Skip Barber Formula Dodge National Championship.

  • 2004–2005

    Super late models on the ovals.

  • 2011

    Back in the Spec Racer Ford for good. Rocky Mountain Division National points champion.

  • 2012

    SCCA SRF National Champion, Road America, with a last-corner pass for the win. Road Racing Drivers Club Mark Donohue Award.

  • 2014

    National Champion again, Laguna Seca, the closest Runoffs finish the class had seen. SCCA Presidents Cup finalist.

  • 2015

    National Champion a third time, at Daytona, by a nose at the line. Southeast Division Driver of the Year.

  • 2016

    Crosses the line first in Spec Racer Ford Gen3 at the Runoffs at Mid-Ohio, then is disqualified in post-race tech. In the paddock it is still “mufflergate.”

  • March 2017

    Wins both Spec Racer Ford Gen3 races at the SCCA Super Tour at Circuit of the Americas, with more than seventy cars on the grid.

  • 2019

    B2 buys a TA2 car to give young Spec Racer drivers a professional shot.

  • 2020

    The car's first Trans Am TA2 start, Sebring, with Cliff driving: 17th on the grid, 15th at the flag, and a long list of lessons. Franklin Futrelle takes over the seat at Road Atlanta in November.

  • March 2021

    Franklin Futrelle gives B2 its first TA2 win, at Road Atlanta, in the car's fifth TA2 start. Second on the grid, the lead on the outside of turn one, held for forty laps, and the fastest lap of the race.

  • May 2021

    Evan Slater's TA2 debut, Lime Rock Park. His third time in the car, the team's first wet sessions ever, and he qualified fifth. A lap down at the finish after mistakes on the pit wall, running top-ten pace. He found a fully funded TA2 ride two years later.

  • 2022

    The shop on Bob Wallace Ave opens, and the focus turns to the Spec Racer community. One SRF in the area.

  • Since 2022

    Steady growth. Four new Spec Racer Ford owners in the Huntsville area, and more than twenty new racers we are helping along the way.

  • 2026

    Arrive-and-drive rentals, coaching, storage and member services from one shop, with the same people at every one of them.

The people behind the cars

The B2 Motorsports team

You are not handed off between weekends. This is the B2 Motorsports team that preps the cars, runs the office and works the radio all season. Cliff and Mark coach for B2, and Evan Slater, Morgan Burkhard and Dave Ogburn are coaches in our network.

Cliff "Boom-Boom" White

Owner, driver coach

Three SCCA SRF national titles (2012, 2014, 2015), the 2012 RRDC Mark Donohue Award, and more than twenty years in the Spec Racer Ford. Works with drivers on weekend and session goals and reads the data with them afterward.

Photo needed: Mark Eaton

Mark Eaton

SRF consultant and driver coach

Mark's résumé goes back to Robinson Motorsports, where he was selling Spec Racers and Formula Enterprises cars at nineteen. In nearly thirty years of Spec Racer Ford he has seen and done it all, including two Formula Enterprises National Championships (2007, 2008) and the 2008 Triple Crown. He consults for the shop, backs up our driver coaching and joins the team on big weekends. Mark is B2 South: based in Melbourne, Florida, with one of our rental cars, close to Daytona and Sebring.

Photo needed: Todd White

Todd White

Crew Chief

Cliff's cousin. More than twenty years in building, maintenance and construction, which is why the shop looks the way it does. The person on the radio when it counts.

Photo needed: Michael Pfeiffer

Michael Pfeiffer

Operations Manager and mechanic

Runs the day-to-day business: bookkeeping, contracts, customer communications, shipping and storage administration, and works on the shop floor and at race weekends wherever he is needed. Michael played baseball from age eight up to Division I at Eastern Michigan University before turning a lifelong passion for motorsports into a job. What he likes most is the people: finding the solution that works, whether a customer needs a place to work on their own car, a spot to store it, or support with a racing program. Five years out, he wants B2 to keep growing without losing the family-owned feel: the go-to shop for SRF3 preparation and support, the best DIY storage and membership garage in Alabama, and the same personal relationships with every customer.

How we work

Three things we will always do

Educate

Classes on alignment, help choosing gear and a used car, one-on-one coaching. Lack of information is the biggest hurdle in this sport; we remove it.

Show the bill

Line-item pricing, our maintenance checklists shared, and a conversation before anything unexpected goes on an invoice.

Talk you out of it

This is an expensive sport. We will actively discourage spending that does not improve safety, performance or the experience.
