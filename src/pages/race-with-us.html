  • ✓The car

    A race-ready Spec Racer Ford Gen3, sealed and equal to every other car on the grid. Prepped before every event: alignment, nut and bolt, wash, driver fitment. More about the car

The day

What a test day actually looks like

Times are from a Chin Track Days weekend at Road Atlanta, advanced solo group. Test days at Polecat run more open track; race weekends add qualifying and the races. The loop is the same everywhere.

  • 7:30

    You arrive. The car is off the trailer, checked and warmed through. You sign in with the organizer and sit in on the drivers' meeting. needs Cliff: the 7:30 arrival and the drivers'-meeting line are inferred from the 9:00 first session, not sourced

  • Before the first session

    The walkaround. Belts and seat, the start-up ritual, the sequential gearbox, what the dash is telling you, how we talk on the radio. You do it, we watch. It is all in the binder in the trailer if you want to read ahead.

  • 9:00

    Session one, twenty minutes. Out-lap to put temperature in the car, then laps. We are on the radio with flags and time remaining.

  • After every session

    Pressures, checks, video. Tire pressures in pit lane, a nut-and-bolt look at the car, then the video and data with a coach, in plain language. One thing to try next time out.

  • Noon, 1:30, 3:00

    Sessions two to four, same loop. Between sessions the awning is yours: shade, seats, something to drink, your people.

  • End of day

    The car goes back on the trailer. You go home with the video, and we settle tires and fuel on what you actually used.

Questions

Three questions everyone asks

Do I need a racing license?

Not for a test day or a track day. Time trials need a Time Trials license, a quick online application with no racing school. Wheel-to-wheel racing needs an SCCA competition license or novice permit. The calendar marks every event.

What happens if I crash?

The $6,000 crash-damage deposit covers repairs and comes back in full if the car is undamaged. Normal wear is ours, not yours. The full policy is on the FAQ. needs Cliff: this crash wording needs business and legal review before it ships
