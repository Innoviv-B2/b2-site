Backlog · prototype, not for launch

The self-service flow, as it could work.

This section and the hall map below are a prototype of a backlog item, kept in the draft so the design can be reviewed. It is not wired up and does not ship until the workflow is designed: someone picks a specific space and registers for that space, or registers for garage membership alone, with availability, DocuSign and payment behind it. backlog: storage sign-up workflow (see thread)

Step 1

Pick a space

Choose an open position on the hall map below, or a trailer or RV space, or the garage on its own. Tell us when you want to start.

Step 2

We confirm it is yours

The map shows what is open right now. We hold the space you picked while the paperwork goes through and confirm within a business day.

Step 3

Sign by DocuSign

The membership agreement arrives by email. Read it, sign it on your phone, nothing to print.

Step 4

Pay, then the door code

First month by ACH. Once it clears you get the door code and the gate access, and the space is yours.
