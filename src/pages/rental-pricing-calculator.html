Tires

The SRF3 runs a spec Hoosier race tire, and we can get as many as 20 heat cycles out of a set. One session is one heat cycle, and the early cycles are worth the most, so new costs more than scuffed. We also have Toyo R888R tires: harder, slightly treaded, and better for track days and non-SCCA events. Pricing is based on Hoosiers.
