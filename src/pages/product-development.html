Scanning & manufacturing

New technologies, better parts, less cost

We use an in-house laser scanner and printer technology to produce higher-performance custom parts, from brake ducts and body shims to drink bottles and simulator pedals. The scanner is a walk-around unit: we move it around the object to capture it, so odd shapes and one-off fitments are no problem. Work happens in our Huntsville shop, at your location, or at the track.

Racing is an expensive sport that takes real expertise to run well. We think the way a race shop earns its keep is by putting that expertise to work for fellow racers and competitors, not just its own fleet.

Why it matters

Parts you actually need, when the paddock does not have them

3D scanning is an efficient way to develop the parts a race weekend actually calls for: SRF body shims, brake ducts, drink bottles, simulator pedals, and other essentials that keep a car legal and on track. Most race events do not carry these in stock, and the ones that do run out. Schedule ahead and we can have what you need ready before you load the trailer.

Who this is for

Racers who value their cars and want a competitive edge

This is built for racers who keep their cars well maintained and want an edge that stock parts cannot give them, whether that is a lighter brake duct, a better-fitting shim, or a scanner and engineer rented out for a weekend of on-site fabrication. It pairs naturally with our shop services: the same crew that preps and repairs your car can design and build the part it is missing.
